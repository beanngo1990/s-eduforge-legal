Account & Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data (including your Name, Email address, and User ID) from the S-RootWords app, please follow one of the methods below.

Method 1: Inside the App (Recommended & Fastest)

You can instantly delete your account directly within the app:

  1. Open the S-RootWords app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings or Profile section.
  3. Tap on the "Delete Account" button.
  4. Confirm your action. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

Method 2: Web Request (Via Email)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request manual data deletion:

  • Send an email to: beanngo1990@gmail.com
  • Important: You must send the email from the exact Google or Apple email address associated with your S-RootWords account.
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - S-RootWords

* Please allow up to 7-14 business days for us to process manual email requests. Once processed, all your personal data and learning progress will be permanently erased.
