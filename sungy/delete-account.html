Account & Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data (including your Email address and User ID) from the Sungy app, please follow one of the methods below.

Method 1: Inside the App (Recommended & Fastest)

You can instantly delete your account directly within the app:

  1. Open the Sungy app on your device.
  2. From the home screen, tap the lock icon (🔒) to open Parent Mode and solve the simple math question.
  3. Scroll down to the "Parent Account" section and make sure you are signed in.
  4. Tap "Delete account" and confirm your action.
  5. Your account and all cloud data will be permanently deleted immediately.

* Note: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Please cancel renewal via App Store or Google Play first if you no longer wish to be charged.

Method 2: Web Request (Via Email)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request manual data deletion:

  • Send an email to: beanngo1990@gmail.com
  • Important: You must send the email from the exact Google or Apple email address associated with your Sungy account.
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Sungy

* Please allow up to 7-14 business days for us to process manual email requests. Once processed, all your personal data and cloud learning progress will be permanently erased.
